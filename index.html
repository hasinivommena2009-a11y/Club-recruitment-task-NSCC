A club built on doing, not just discussing

Vertex started as three friends prototyping a single weekend hackathon project. Today, we're a cross-disciplinary community where engineers, designers, and first-time builders work side by side.

Every semester runs on the same idea: pick something you don't know how to do yet, find a small team, and ship it. Members leave with real projects, real collaborators, and the confidence that comes from having built something from scratch.

What we actually do

Five recurring tracks make up most of the club calendar.

Events & Workshops

Hands-on sessions on everything from Git to hardware soldering, run by members for members.

Technical Projects

Semester-long builds in web, apps, robotics, and AI, in small teams with a real ship date.

Creative Activities

Design jams, film nights, and content crews for members who build with pixels instead of code.

Team Building

Retreats, game nights, and cross-team mixers so collaborators become friends.

Leadership Opportunities

Run a team, lead a workshop, or sit on the core committee — leadership is earned, not seniority-based.
